# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Speedysearch community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized attention,
threats, publication of private information, and other conduct that would
reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation
that do not align with this policy.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the same private channel
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), with “Code of Conduct” in the report
title. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Enforcement may
range from a private correction to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on
severity and pattern.
